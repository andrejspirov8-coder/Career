## Highest-value proof to collect next

1. APM Monaco sales target or achievement example.
2. Any conversion, ATV, UPT, stock-accuracy, shrink, or audit improvement.
3. One quantified automation result, such as minutes saved, manual steps removed, or errors prevented.
4. A public LinkedIn URL and a sanitised GitHub repository or one-page project case study.
5. Exact systems used: POS name, marketplaces, reporting tools, and any real n8n/Zapier workflow.

## Claims boundary

Do not add ARIS, BPMN, Lean Six Sigma, DMAIC, UAT, SLA ownership, CRM/ERP administration, Jira, ServiceNow, SQL, cloud infrastructure, or network support without a specific example that can be defended in an interview.

For n8n and Zapier, use “working knowledge” until a real workflow can be described with its trigger, actions, data handled, error path, and outcome.
